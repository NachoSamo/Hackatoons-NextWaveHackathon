## Benchmark sources and claim boundaries

- Yuno Monitors publicly describes real-time anomaly detection, alerts and automatic traffic
redistribution: [https://www.y.uno/en/product/monitors](https://www.y.uno/en/product/monitors).
- Yuno Payments Concierge publicly describes anomaly detection, conversational reporting and
governed operational actions across channels: [https://y.uno/en/newsroom/yuno-launches-payments-concierge](https://y.uno/en/newsroom/yuno-launches-payments-concierge).
- Primer Observability describes curated and custom payment dashboards for analyzing payment flows:
[https://primer.io/docs/observability/overview](https://primer.io/docs/observability/overview).
- Spreedly AI Analytics describes natural-language payment exploration, anomaly summaries and
gateway-level failure analysis: [https://developer.spreedly.com/docs/ai-analytics](https://developer.spreedly.com/docs/ai-analytics).
- Datadog Watchdog describes automated baselines, anomaly detection and root-cause analysis across
general infrastructure observability: [https://docs.datadoghq.com/watchdog/](https://docs.datadoghq.com/watchdog/).

The slide does **not** claim that competitors cannot build Centinel's features. It identifies the
product white space explored by this prototype: payment-domain incident localization, an explicit
evidence contract, visible uncertainty and a human-reviewed handoff.

## Demo decision



### Primary: recorded demo

The recorded path compresses waiting time while preserving one continuous execution: healthy
silence, an injected but not pre-diagnosed slice, deterministic localization and a traceable
evidence contract. Samo and Oso own the edit, playback and narration.

### Post-pitch proof: live trial by fire

At 06:45 Juani invites the judges to choose any valid combination that was not rehearsed. The live
injection begins only after the seven-minute close. Keep the local Command Center reset and ready;
the trial proves generalization without putting the pitch chronology at risk.

## Speaking contract

- Pennazi opens, explains the architecture/data world and returns for the production path.
- Juani owns orchestrator positioning, product value, benchmark and the final invitation.
- Samo owns healthy state, injection, detection, stacked incidents and the executive-view beat.
- Oso owns filtering, diagnosis and temporal comparison; Samo and Oso close the video together.
- Juani keeps the Slack/action beat already assigned in the original speaker column.
- No handoff starts with a personal introduction. The previous speaker ends by setting up the next idea.



## Rehearsal gates

- **Green:** live path succeeds three consecutive times and finishes by 06:05.
- **Yellow:** one flaky external dependency; keep live but prove the local deterministic fallback.
- **Red:** core stream, injection or state transitions fail twice on the final rehearsal; lead with the
continuous recording and retain only the judge-controlled injection as an optional post-pitch proof.
